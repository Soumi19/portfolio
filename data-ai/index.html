Which data analysis and research tasks can you support?

I support data cleaning, exploratory analysis, survey statistics, forecasting, data visualisation, dashboard development and applied machine learning. The method is chosen around the research question, dataset and reporting requirements.

My toolkit includes Python, R, SQL, Power BI, Tableau, IBM SPSS and Excel. Depending on the brief, I use Pandas, NumPy, scikit-learn, Matplotlib or ggplot2 for preparation, modelling and visualisation.

What evidence of your data and AI work is available?

My project portfolio includes computer vision, anomaly detection, time-series forecasting and business dashboards. Public GitHub repositories provide source code and documentation where available.
