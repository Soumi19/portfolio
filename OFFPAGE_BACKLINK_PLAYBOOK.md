# Off-page SEO, backlinks & AI-citation playbook

See BACKLINK_ACTION_LIST.md for the paste-ready steps. This page explains the strategy.

## 1. Entity consistency
Use the same name, descriptor and link everywhere: Soumi Ganguly, freelance technical content writer, plus the website URL and the same location wording (Howrah, West Bengal, India, working remotely). Consistency helps Google and AI assistants connect the mentions to one person.

## 2. Profiles you own
LinkedIn, Medium, GitHub, Muck Rack: add the website link and the standard bio.

## 3. Earn editorial links
Guest articles, expert quotes, resource-page suggestions and client mentions carry far more weight than directory profiles.

## 4. Get named in AI answers
AI assistants favour pages that answer a question directly, stay fresh and are mentioned on several trusted sites. Keep the Quick-answer boxes and FAQs accurate, update the "Last updated" date when content changes, and aim for mentions on at least three independent sites.

## 5. Reviews and testimonials
Add real client feedback with permission using data/testimonials.json and `node scripts/add-testimonials.mjs`. Do not add star ratings or review markup for your own site.

## 6. Avoid
Bought links, private blog networks, mass directory submissions, comment spam, fake reviews, AI-spun guest posts.

## 7. Track monthly
Google Search Console (queries by country: India, UK, USA), Bing Webmaster Tools, referring domains (Ahrefs Webmaster Tools is free), and whether assistants name you when asked "who is a freelance technical content writer in India".
