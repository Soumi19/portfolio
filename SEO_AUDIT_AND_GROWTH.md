# SEO, AEO and long-term discovery

## Implemented on site
- Crawlable multi-page HTML; portable asset paths; one canonical and meta description per page.
- Page-specific service intent, descriptive headings, internal service links and direct contact CTAs.
- Person, Article and Service structured data retained; breadcrumbs on research service pages; visible question-and-answer content for answer-oriented search.
- XML sitemap, image sitemap, robots.txt and configurable HTTPS origin.
- Lazy-loaded content images, bundled fonts/assets, reduced-motion support and background suspension on hidden tabs.
- No invented overseas office addresses, client results, review ratings or search-volume metrics.

## Off-page work requiring your participation
1. Add the final website URL to your real LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium and Muck Rack profiles.
2. Ask genuine collaborators for relevant portfolio credits and editorial links where they have permission to publish them.
3. Publish original research explainers and documented development projects, then share them with relevant communities under their posting rules.
4. Seek editorial mentions through useful contributions; avoid bought link networks, fabricated testimonials and mass directory submissions.
5. Verify Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools and submit the sitemap. Use actual query data to refine pages and identify demand.
6. Review enquiries and query performance monthly; update examples and service descriptions as your work develops.

No external backlinks, account verifications or search-volume research were performed. First-page rankings, AI-answer inclusion and FAQ rich results are not guaranteed. Core Web Vitals need measurement on the deployed site; this package has not received a Lighthouse score or browser visual certification.

## Font and motion
Body text and headings use Times New Roman with Times/Liberation Serif/Georgia fallbacks. System serif fonts do not add network requests. Cards, buttons and article imagery have restrained hover transitions; existing scroll reveals and silver background motion remain. Reduced-motion settings disable decorative movement.
