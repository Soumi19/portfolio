answered.

Which digital marketing services do you offer?

I offer SEO-focused website copy, content strategy, editorial planning, articles and digital campaign content support. The brief is shaped around the audience, search intent and business goals.

Which tools are relevant to a digital marketing brief?

Depending on the scope and available accounts, digital marketing may use Google Analytics 4, Google Search Console, Google Ads, Meta Business Suite and WordPress. Tool selection follows the actual reporting and content requirements.

Can you write technical marketing content?

Yes. My technical writing background supports research-led content for software, AI, data and other technology subjects. The content explains the topic clearly while addressing the audience’s questions.

What is SEO content writing?

SEO content writing creates pages and articles that answer what people search for, using a clear structure, relevant terms and accurate information so both readers and search engines can understand the page.

How is SEO content different from social media content?

SEO content is built to be found through search and stays useful over time. Social content is built for feeds and conversation and is usually shorter-lived. A plan often uses both.

What goes into an editorial plan?

An editorial plan lists audience, topics, search intent, formats, publishing dates and review steps, so content is produced consistently instead of ad hoc.

Questions before you enquire

Does digital marketing include social media branding?

The services can be combined, but the scope differs. Marketing covers audience acquisition and campaigns; social branding focuses on identity, tone and a consistent content presence.

Can you guarantee Google’s first position?

No. Search positions depend on competition, relevance, technical factors and search systems outside a service provider’s control.

What should I share before discussing a campaign?

Share your offer, target audience, website, current channels, objective and any budget or timing constraints.
