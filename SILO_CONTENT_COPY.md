## /student-data-analysis/

Title: SPSS, R & Python Research Analysis Support | Soumi Ganguly

Description: Research data analysis support using SPSS, R, Python and Excel: data preparation, statistical analysis, visualisation and interpretation.

# Turn research data into findings you can explain.


### Start with the research question

I help organise quantitative analysis around the questions your study needs to answer. Depending on the project, support can include data checks, descriptive statistics, reliability assessment, hypothesis testing, regression and visualisation.


### Select methods for the data you actually have

SPSS, R, Python and Excel serve different workflows. Method selection depends on variable types, sampling, assumptions and research design. A result should be explained with its uncertainty and limitations; significance alone does not establish practical importance or causation.


### What to share for a scope review

Send an anonymised dataset, questionnaire or variable guide, research questions and the output format required. Identify any mandatory software. The analysis plan and deliverables are agreed before work begins.


### Can you analyse survey data in SPSS?

Yes. Survey support can include coding, data screening, descriptive tables, reliability checks and suitable inferential methods.


### Will you improve a low reliability score by changing responses?

No. Responses should not be altered to produce a preferred outcome. Low reliability can be investigated and reported transparently.


### What outputs can I request?

Depending on the brief, outputs may include analysis files, tables, charts and explanations of the results and limitations.
