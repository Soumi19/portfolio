What can a customised CRM include?

A customised CRM can include customer records, lead pipelines, tasks, follow-up workflows, role-based dashboards and reporting views. The agreed scope reflects your business process and existing systems.

How is the CRM scope agreed?

We first map the workflow, user roles and information requirements. The brief defines the features, technical responsibilities, review criteria and documentation before development begins.

Can CRM development connect with website development?

Yes. CRM requirements can be discussed alongside a website or web application brief, with interfaces and data flows planned around the agreed business workflow.
