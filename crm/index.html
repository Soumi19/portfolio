A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Your questions,
answered.

What can a customised CRM include?

A customised CRM can include customer records, lead pipelines, tasks, follow-up workflows, role-based dashboards and reporting views. The agreed scope reflects your business process and existing systems.

How is the CRM scope agreed?

We first map the workflow, user roles and information requirements. The brief defines the features, technical responsibilities, review criteria and documentation before development begins.

Can CRM development connect with website development?

Yes. CRM requirements can be discussed alongside a website or web application brief, with interfaces and data flows planned around the agreed business workflow.

What is a custom CRM?

A custom CRM is customer relationship management software built around how your team actually works, tracking customer records, leads, follow-up tasks and reports in one place instead of forcing your process into a generic tool.

Do I need a CRM or is a spreadsheet enough?

A spreadsheet is enough while one or two people track a few contacts. A CRM becomes useful when several people need shared records, follow-up reminders, permissions and reports.

What can a custom CRM track?

Typical items are customer details, enquiry sources, lead stages, follow-up tasks, notes and sales or service reports. The fields and stages are defined from your workflow map.
