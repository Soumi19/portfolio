# Backlink action list (ready to paste)

Backlinks must be created on other websites by you, from accounts only you can open, so they cannot be added by editing this site. Below is what to do and exactly what to paste. Replace SITE-URL with your final domain.

**Standard lines to reuse everywhere**
- One line: Soumi Ganguly is a freelance technical content writer and data analyst in India. SITE-URL
- Short bio: Soumi Ganguly is a freelance technical content writer based in Howrah, West Bengal, India, with 4+ years of experience. She writes research-led articles, documentation and reports, and supports data analytics, websites and customised CRM workflows for clients in India and abroad. SITE-URL
- Anchor text variety (rotate): "Soumi Ganguly", "freelance technical writer", "technical content writer in India", "SPSS data analysis help".

**Do first (about one hour, free)**
1. LinkedIn: Contact info > Website; add the one-line bio to Headline or About; add the site to Featured.
2. Medium: bio line + a closing line on every article: "More work at SITE-URL".
3. GitHub: profile README with the one-line bio and link; add the site URL in each pinned repo's "Website" field.
4. Muck Rack: website field and bio.
5. Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster Tools: verify the final domain and submit sitemap.xml.

**Do next (free profile listings, mainly brand and trust signals)**
Upwork, Fiverr, Contra, Freelancer.com, PeoplePerHour (only the ones you will really use), Dev.to or Hashnode (a technical article with an author-bio link), About.me, Behance (for design or writing samples). Most profile links are nofollow, so their value is visibility, referrals and consistent identity rather than ranking power.

**Links that move rankings most (editorial links)**
- Guest articles on data science, EdTech, research-skills and technical-writing blogs, with an author-bio link.
- University library or research-skills resource pages: suggest a free explainer only where it fits.
- Expert-quote requests (journalist platforms such as Qwoted or Featured): answer on data, SPSS, dissertations and technical writing.
- Ask clients for a link when they publish work you wrote or analysed.

**Outreach email template**
Subject: Guest article idea for [Site]
Hi [Name], I'm Soumi Ganguly, a freelance technical content writer. I enjoyed [specific article]. I'd like to propose "[title]", which would help your readers [benefit]. My work is at SITE-URL. Would you like an outline first? Thank you, Soumi

**Avoid:** bought links, private blog networks, mass directory submission, comment spam, fake reviews.
