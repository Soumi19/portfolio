Quick answer

What is SPSS, R and Python research analysis support?

It is help with analysing research data: checking a dataset, running statistical methods that suit your research question in SPSS, R or Python, presenting the results in readable tables and charts, and explaining what the output means. Methods follow your data and assumptions; significant results cannot be promised.

  • Covers: Dataset checks, descriptive statistics, reliability, correlations, regression guidance, visualisation, interpretation
  • Tools: SPSS, R, Python, Excel, Tableau and Power BI
  • Receive: Prepared variables, readable tables and charts, plain-language interpretation of your output
  • Related: data analytics and AI, dissertation support

Understand your data and explain your findings clearly.

Good analysis starts with the right method for the question, the variables and the sampling design. Support focuses on accurate methods, transparent reporting and defensible interpretation.

IN PLAIN WORDS

Statistics terms in plain words.

Descriptive statistics

Summaries of your data such as averages and spread, which show what the dataset looks like.

Reliability

Whether a questionnaire or scale gives consistent results.

Regression

A method for estimating how one outcome relates to one or more other factors.

Hypothesis test

A check on whether a pattern in your sample is likely to reflect something real rather than chance.

A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Your questions,
answered.

Which tools can I discuss?

SPSS, R, Python, Excel, Tableau and Power BI are included in the portfolio. Share your required software and method when contacting Soumi.

Can you guarantee statistically significant findings?

No. Findings depend on the data and appropriate analysis. Support focuses on accurate methods, transparent reporting and defensible interpretation.

What data format should I send?

Send the dataset in the format you have, for example an Excel or CSV file or an SPSS file, along with a short description of each variable.

Will you explain the results to me?

Yes. Interpretation of SPSS, R or Python output is part of the support, so you can explain the findings in your own report.

I only need charts. Is that possible?

Yes. You can ask for readable research visualisations on their own. Share the data and the message each chart should carry.

Do you offer SPSS help for dissertation data analysis?

Yes. SPSS support covers checking your dataset, choosing methods that suit the research question, running the tests and explaining the output so you can report the results yourself.

Which statistical tests can you guide me on?

Common tests include t-tests, chi-square tests, correlation, regression and reliability analysis, alongside descriptive statistics and visualisation. The right test depends on your variables and assumptions.
