2026–Present

MBA in Operations Management

IGNOU · Indira Gandhi National Open University

View five key points +
  • In plain words: An MBA in Operations Management teaches how organisations run smoothly: planning work, managing processes and resources, and leading teams. IGNOU is the Indira Gandhi National Open University, one of India’s open and distance universities.
  • What it covers: Business operations, organisational processes and management. This programme is ongoing (2026–present).
  • How it adds to a technical background: It pairs technical knowledge with a business view of how work flows through an organisation, which helps when gathering requirements for a project.
  • Skills it builds: Process thinking, planning and organisation, and an understanding of what stakeholders need from a report, a website or a system.
  • How it helps a client: Technical writing, analytics and CRM or website work can be shaped around real business workflows, not only technical features.
2022–2026

B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering (Data Science)

MCKV Institute of Engineering

View five key points +
  • In plain words: A B.Tech is a four-year engineering degree. This one is in computer science with a data science focus: how computers store data, analyse it and learn patterns from it.
  • What it covers: Data science technology, machine learning and computer vision foundations.
  • Where it shows up: In hands-on work such as real-time object detection, AI-driven cheat detection and rainfall trend analysis, which you can browse on the projects page.
  • Tools and methods: Python, R and SQL for working with data, plus libraries such as Pandas, scikit-learn, OpenCV and PyTorch.
  • How it helps a client: Data analysis, forecasting and AI topics can be explained accurately and turned into clear reports, dashboards and technical content.
2019–2022

Diploma in Computer Science Engineering

SCM Institute of Engineering and Technology

View five key points +
  • In plain words: A diploma in computer science engineering is a three-year technical programme (2019–2022) that teaches the practical basics of computers and programming.
  • What it covers: A foundation in programming, computing and technical problem-solving.
  • Why it matters: It was the first step of a technical education, the base on which later data science and web development work was built.
  • Skills it built: Reading and writing code, breaking a problem into steps and testing a solution until it works.
  • How it helps a client: Writing about software and systems is more accurate when it comes from having worked with them directly.

Statistical analysis and research tools.
