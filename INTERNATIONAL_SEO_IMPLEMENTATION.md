# International SEO implementation

Implemented in the site package:
- crawlable static HTML and internal links
- canonical URLs and index/follow directives
- XML sitemap + image sitemap + robots.txt
- Person, WebSite and FAQ structured data where applicable
- descriptive page titles/descriptions and social metadata
- readable semantic headings and accessible image alt handling
- international-services page and explicit international collaboration copy
- performance-friendly lazy loading for non-hero images
- high-contrast responsive UI and reduced-motion support

## International targeting strategy
Do not create dozens of near-duplicate country doorway pages. The site should earn international visibility through useful service pages, portfolio proof, authoritative mentions and links. For genuine country-specific pages, publish materially different content (currency/process/time-zone/case studies/FAQs) and then use country/language hreflang.

Priority markets requested: United Kingdom, United States, Kuwait and New Zealand, plus broader international remote work. Add genuine country-specific case studies/testimonials as they become available.

## Off-page work that cannot be completed inside a ZIP
1. Verify the live domain in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.
2. Submit sitemap.xml and image-sitemap.xml.
3. Keep LinkedIn, GitHub, Medium, Muck Rack and other author profiles consistent with the same name, services and canonical portfolio URL.
4. Earn editorial links from real publications, universities, professional directories and client/partner sites. Do not buy links or use link schemes.
5. Publish original case studies and articles that can earn citations and AI-search references.
6. Use IndexNow/Bing Webmaster tools after important updates when supported by the hosting setup.
7. Monitor country-level queries, impressions, CTR and indexed pages, then expand pages based on actual demand.
