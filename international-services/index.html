Where is Soumi Ganguly based?

Soumi Ganguly is based in Howrah, West Bengal, India. She welcomes remote technical writing, data analytics and digital development enquiries from India, the UK, the USA and other international markets.

Can we work together remotely from another country?

Yes. Share your project goals, audience and preferred time zone through the contact form. Communication channels, review stages and any meeting times are agreed before work starts.

Which services are available for international projects?

Services include technical content writing, research communication, data analytics, website and web application development, customised CRM, digital marketing and social media branding. Each brief is assessed for scope and feasibility.

What should I include in my project enquiry?

Include your objective, target audience, required deliverables, available source material or data, preferred timeline, budget range and relevant technical constraints. This helps define a realistic scope.
