A FEW USEFUL ANSWERS

Your questions,
answered.

What does your social media branding service include?

Social media branding includes brand voice, profile copy, content pillars, post planning, captions and guidance on visual consistency. Deliverables are agreed around the platforms, audience and communication goals.

Which tools support social content planning?

Canva can support creative production, Meta Business Suite can support Meta platform publishing, and Buffer can support scheduling. The agreed workflow determines which tools and accounts are needed.

Can social branding align with my website content?

Yes. Brand messaging, website copy and social content can follow the same voice and content direction, helping people recognise your business across its digital channels.

What is a content pillar?

A content pillar is a core theme your brand returns to regularly, such as education, behind-the-scenes or customer stories. Pillars keep posts consistent and make planning easier.

How often should a small business post on social media?

It depends on your capacity. Consistency matters more than volume, so the plan should be manageable enough to keep up, with a schedule agreed around your platforms and audience.

What is a brand voice?

Brand voice is the personality and tone of your messages, for example warm, expert or playful. A defined voice makes posts, captions and replies feel like they come from the same brand.
